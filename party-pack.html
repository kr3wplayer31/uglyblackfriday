HOW GROUP ORDERING WORKS HERE

Four Steps. Zero Herding Cats.

1

Share The One Link

Send your crew the collection link: shop.artificial-intelligencenow.com/collections/ugly-christmas-sweaters. That's the whole catalog — our own designs, the sweater shop, all of it.

2

Everyone Picks Their Own

Each person chooses the design that fits THEIR personality — the forklift guy gets Forklift Santa, the cat person gets Merry Chaos. Matching is allowed. Matching is not required.

3

Collect Names & Sizes

Use the sign-up sheet generator below. Print it for the break room, or copy the rows into your group chat. Name, size, design — that's all you need from each person.

4

One Checkout, One Code

The organizer places one order with everyone's picks. Group of 5 or more sweaters? Enter the group code at checkout (see below). Then somebody books the party photo.
